Wedding photography

From €950

This package covers six hours by your side: the ceremony, the formal photos and the drinks reception. If you would like me to be there from the moment you start getting ready, or to stay on into the evening, I simply add €100 for each extra hour.

There are the moments you expect, and all those that slip in between them: a gesture of the hand, a moved glance among the guests, reunions, bursts of laughter. These details, as much as the big moments, are what give your wedding its story.

Before the big day, we choose together the moments you would like to entrust to me, from the ceremony alone to the whole day. On the day itself, I photograph what happens, without staging anything, so that you are free to live your day. If you would like, we also plan a moment for just the two of you, away from the celebrations, for a few couple portraits.

What’s included

  • Around a hundred photographs per hour of coverage

How does it work?

  1. A first message

    You write to me, even if your plans are only just taking shape. I reply within 48 hours.

  2. Getting to know each other

    Over the phone or by text message, we talk about your story, the place and what you would like to keep in pictures. For a wedding, I’m always happy to suggest meeting up over a coffee. It is also the moment to ask all your questions.

  3. The big day, or the session

    I adapt to your pace and stay discreet. For a session, everything begins with a few steps together, just long enough for the camera to fade into the background.

  4. Your photographs

    You receive them within four weeks, in colour and in black and white, in a private gallery that is yours alone. You can download them in web size as well as in high resolution, to share, print or give as gifts: you are free to use them however you like.

A few frequently asked questions

Do we need to know how to pose?

No. I guide you at the beginning, then the session gradually gives way to your conversations and your gestures.

Can we plan couple photos on the wedding day?

Yes. Together we choose a moment that fits in with the flow of the day, for example between the ceremony and the celebration, so that you can enjoy it without being away from your guests for too long.

Do you travel outside Alsace?

Yes, gladly, depending on the project. For travel costs outside Alsace, I prepare a quote for you: just write to me to talk about it.

How far in advance should we book?

The earlier the better, to make sure I’m free on your date. But if in doubt, just ask me!
