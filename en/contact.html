Are you planning a wedding, or would you like some photographs of the two of you? You are welcome to write to me even if your plans are only just beginning to take shape.

Write to me

To tell me about your plans or to ask me a question, just send me an email. I reply within 48 hours.

hello@lucathis.art

I’m based in Alsace, near Saverne, and I travel throughout Alsace and further afield depending on the project.

Let’s talk about what you have in mind

Simply tell me what brings you here. A few pointers for a good start:

  • The occasion: a wedding, an engagement, a session for two
  • The date and the venue, if you already know them, especially for a wedding
  • For a session, what you would like and the time of year you have in mind
  • The people who will be there, a place that means a lot to you, what you would like to find in your pictures

You can also share your questions or your hesitations with me, including if you don’t feel very comfortable in front of a camera. We can talk about how a session goes and imagine together what would suit you.

What happens next?

After your first message, we get to know each other over the phone or by text message. For a wedding, I’m always happy to suggest meeting up over a coffee.

Follow my work day by day

On Instagram, I also share my latest stories and a few moments from my everyday life as a photographer. There you will find the encounters, the places and the light that inspire the way I see.
