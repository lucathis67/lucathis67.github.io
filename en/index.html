Strasbourg’s Cathedral of Notre-Dame, the historic treasure of the Alsatian capital, is a monument familiar to everyone who visits the city. It is also a living place of worship, rooted in the parish life of the people who live here. The Cathedral offers a splendid setting, majestic and solemn, in which to celebrate a sacrament as beautiful and as meaningful as marriage.

From behind my camera, I was discovering this ceremony from a unique perspective. The rhythm of the traditional Catholic liturgy, the priest’s gestures, the colours and the light of the Cathedral gave some scenes the look of great paintings from the classical era.

On their wedding day, between the ceremony and the celebration, Laure and Nicolas took a moment for a few couple photos in Strasbourg. I went along with them for this short session. A few steps together, glances exchanged, tender gestures: a pause in a big day spent surrounded by their loved ones.

When you are planning your wedding, you imagine the reunions and the joy of bringing together the people you love. You sometimes think less about the time you will have to simply be the two of you.

Through Laure and Nicolas’s photographs, I share how a couple session can find its place in the day, so that you can take time to be together and keep the memory of it.

A few months before their wedding, Julie and Adrien met up in the Parc de l’Orangerie in Strasbourg for an engagement session. It was the height of autumn, and the trees in the park were showing off their most spectacular colours!

A walk for two… which I discreetly joined, camera in hand. As the pictures went on, the session gradually moved away from traditional poses to make room for glances, tender gestures and the closeness they share.
