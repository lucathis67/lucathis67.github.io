I’m a wedding and couple photographer based near Saverne in Alsace. I love photographing the bonds between people: a way of looking at each other, a familiar gesture, a shared moment that you will be glad to find again a few years later.

Photography, one encounter at a time

I discovered photography more than ten years ago with Leslie Maron, an American wedding photographer who taught me its artistic and technical foundations.

Since then, my camera has come with me on my travels, to my encounters and through the different chapters of my life. It has often given me the chance to get to know people I might never have met otherwise. It has also taught me to look more attentively at the world around me.

Over the years, my own photographs have taken on an importance I did not always appreciate when I took them. In them I find faces, places, moments of life. This experience shapes the care I put into the images I create for you today.

Taking the time to get to know each other

Being photographed doesn’t always come naturally, and I completely understand that. That’s why, before a wedding or a session, I like to take the time to talk with you, to discover your story and what you would like to keep from this moment.

During a couple session, it might begin with a walk and a conversation. Little by little, the camera takes up less space and gestures become more spontaneous. I stay attentive to what is happening between you, while guiding you whenever you need it.

At a wedding, this same attention guides my eye: I follow the course of the day, the people around you, the moments you expect and those that happen without warning. I try to write a story in pictures in which you can recognise yourselves.

Photographs that stand the test of time

Every now and then, at a friend’s home, I come across a photograph I took long ago, in a frame or as a screensaver. It always touches me to see that an image has found its place in their everyday life.

That is what I hope for yours, too: that they stay with you and that, when you look at them, you find something of what you lived. The atmosphere of a day, the presence of someone dear, your very own way of being together at that time in your life.

Whether it is a wedding or a walk for two, I bring the same attention to it, with the wish to create images that feel like you and that you will love coming back to.
