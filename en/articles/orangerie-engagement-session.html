Finding the right moment, with the wedding on the horizon

I met Julie and Adrien in the Parc de l’Orangerie in Strasbourg for their engagement session. On this glorious autumn day, the trees had turned yellow and orange, creating a particularly warm setting for this walk for two.

An engagement session, sometimes called a pre-wedding shoot, usually takes place anywhere from a few months to a year before the wedding. It is a rather special time: the big day is starting to get closer, while still leaving enough time to enjoy it without feeling overtaken by the preparations.

When the wedding is in summer, the engagement session can take place the autumn before, when the colours of the parks make for a particularly photogenic setting.

Relaxing in front of the camera

The wedding day is often the first time you are photographed by a professional for several hours. An engagement session is a great way to break the ice, so that you feel more at ease in front of the camera on your wedding day. And it is much easier than you might think! During this kind of session, sometimes just a few minutes are enough to forget about the camera and for trust to grow between the couple and the photographer. With Julie and Adrien, the session gradually turned into a simple walk for two…

Building a bond with your photographer

This time spent with the photographer is also a calm moment, ideal for getting to know each other better. The photographer is a rather unusual witness at a wedding: their role is to tell a story in pictures. I truly believe that this story is about more than pretty photos: it should feel like you, as if it were told by someone who really knows you. That is also why I like to take the time to get to know the couples I photograph. With Julie and Adrien, this session let me discover their closeness and their story before the wedding day itself.

The time of a solemn promise

Before it is a big celebration, a wedding is first and foremost a commitment between two people. A very solemn decision in the course of a life. I think this time of engagement, when you decide to give yourself to the other, is just as important as the day the wedding is celebrated. An engagement session captures in pictures this very strong bond that exists, above all, between the two of you.

Precious memories for the couple

In some of the pictures from this session, the engagement ring appears almost as a detail. And yet it says a great deal: behind this object lies a decision, the decision to build something together. Engagement photographs can bring the symbolism of this promise to life, and capture the full range of emotions that belong to these moments.

Photos to share with your loved ones

The photos can also find their place in the wedding preparations: to announce the date, to illustrate an invitation or simply to share a few beautiful pictures with your loved ones.

But their value doesn’t stop there. In a few years’ time, they will also preserve the memory of that precise moment when you were still engaged, a few months before becoming a married couple: a visual record of the anticipation and excitement surrounding this new chapter of your story.
