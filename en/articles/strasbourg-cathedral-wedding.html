An exceptional place steeped in art and history

Wherever you look, masterpieces of Christian inspiration intertwine in an endless fresco. Beneath the coloured light of the stained-glass windows, Gothic and Romanesque architecture meet, while paintings and finely carved woodwork come together to play a silent symphony that has lasted for more than 800 years in this remarkable place.

A solemn and timeless atmosphere

Beneath the ribbed vaults, you can feel the weight of history. Every gesture of the ceremony echoes a tradition repeated down the centuries by so many other couples who sealed their lifelong commitment here.

The splendour of the Catholic liturgy

From behind my camera, I was discovering this ceremony from a unique perspective. The rhythm of the traditional Catholic liturgy, the priest’s gestures, the colours and the light of the Cathedral gave some scenes the look of great paintings from the classical era.

Love, faith and commitment

The beauty of the place, the words exchanged and the Gregorian chant that accompanied the ceremony gave this commitment a particular depth. For Laure and Nicolas, their wedding seemed to express fully the place that love and faith hold in their lives.

A ray of hope

Just after the exchange of rings, a ray of sunlight shone through the rose window, lighting up the choir and wrapping the newlyweds in a halo of sparkling white light. An unexpected moment, almost suspended in time, that I could never have staged.

I saw in it a lovely metaphor for what marriage can stand for: a message of hope, both a promise and a grace that finds its place at the heart of our everyday lives.

How do you prepare for a wedding at Strasbourg Cathedral?

Weddings at Strasbourg Cathedral take place in the crypt or in the Chapel of Saint Lawrence, not in the great nave, which remains open to the public. The chapel can welcome around a hundred people and the crypt up to 250, according to the information provided by the parish. If you are thinking of getting married there, contact the parish office well in advance to find out how things are organised, the steps to take and the available dates.
