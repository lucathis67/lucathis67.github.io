A setting of colour

Summer may be my favourite season for its long days, but I have to admit that autumn offers the most spectacular colours, perfect for an outdoor couple session! For a few weeks, nature gives us a true open-air jewel box. Here are some of the memories created for Tanguy and Mathilde on a beautiful autumn afternoon in Strasbourg.

A walk beneath the urban canopy

Rather than a series of scenes and complicated poses, I prefer the session to unfold like a walk with friends, where the camera is little more than an accessory. The most beautiful moments always come after a few minutes spent getting to know each other as we walk. Little by little, you begin to forget the camera is there.

Telling a love story

In a world that moves so fast, a photo session is also a moment of calm, a pause for two, sheltered from the bustle of everyday life. Taking the time to walk hand in hand, to tell each other a story, to look at nature, to listen.

This time also builds trust between us, and it shows in images that are much truer to who you are. The natural beauty of the closeness and love that unite Mathilde and Tanguy can be read in their eyes and in their smiles.

Quite simply

For this lovely couple, the day was also a chance to celebrate a happy event to come! A tiny little boy playing hide-and-seek in his mum’s tummy.
Whether it is a pregnancy, or simply the wish to keep a few beautiful pictures of your life together, I believe a love story is best told simply: not by trying to create perfect memories, but by giving emotions all the room they need to come through.

I also believe in the value photos take on as the years go by: that one day we may love being able to see ourselves again, to find the faces of those we love, and to truly recognise ourselves in every detail. In the way we look at each other, in the little gestures whose meaning is sometimes known only to the other, like a secret code.

Of course, autumn is not just a display of colours. It is also a slightly nostalgic time of year, when the days grow shorter and we rediscover the comfort of simple things before the festive season arrives. A season that makes you want to draw closer, to share a little warmth, and why not treat yourselves to a few memories together.

What if autumn were simply the perfect season for a couple session?

With its warm colours, its golden light and its still-mild temperatures, autumn is an invitation to go for a walk. In Strasbourg, as in this session in the gardens of the Neustadt, and everywhere else in Alsace, parks and green spaces become so many settings for wonderful photos.
There is absolutely no need to know how to pose or to be used to the camera. All it takes is the wish to share a moment together, to walk a little, and to go with the flow.
